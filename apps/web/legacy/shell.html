About this prototype

Works in this prototype

  • Placement search: every carport position on a 0.5 m grid, in both orientations (opening to the front/back or to the side).
  • Programmed rule checks with an explanation and source for each result.
  • Vehicle check: the full area the car sweeps (6.5 m outer turning radius, 0.5 m clearance), a straight entry into the bay, and for a double carport each car with the other bay occupied.
  • What-if: drag and resize a footprint; the checks update.

Simulated or still to validate

  • Property data is fictional. There is no live link to BAG or Kadaster yet.
  • Rule coverage is a sample modelled on Gemeente Woensdrecht, not verified with Vergunklaar.
  • The vehicle model is a provisional assumption, not validated against a standard or real drives. The manoeuvre on the public road is not checked.
  • Zoning, floor heights and underground services are assumed or not checked.

Why a carport first

The planned rear-extension pilot depends on whether a house was extended before, which public data doesn't show. A carport can be checked from the plot alone, and it tests something an extension doesn't: whether a car can actually reach it. The same engine (placement search, rule checks, explanations) carries over to ground-floor extensions and other outbuildings next.
